# Fellow Onboarding Checklist

> Complete together in the first two weeks. Keep contact and payment details in the agreed private system, not in this repository.

## Program team confirms

- [ ] Primary mentor or program lead, fallback contact, and check-in cadence are confirmed.
- [ ] Fellowship dates, track, expected hours, stipend, and payment process are written in the signed agreement.
- [ ] Communication channels and response expectations are clear.
- [ ] The fellow has received the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), [Fellow Handbook](../FELLOW_HANDBOOK.md), and privacy/reporting expectations.
- [ ] A safe reporting location is agreed for weekly updates, monthly updates, and educator session logs.

## Fellow confirms

- [ ] I understand the work plan and know what is in and out of scope for Month 1.
- [ ] I know how to contact my mentor/program lead and fallback contact.
- [ ] I have a safe way to share updates and ask for help.
- [ ] I have reviewed upstream or venue/community rules relevant to my work.
- [ ] I have shared payment details privately and understand the payment timing.
