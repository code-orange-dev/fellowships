# Educator Fellowship Reference

This page explains the session-credit system. Start with the [Educator Fellowship](README.md) and use the [Fellow Handbook](../FELLOW_HANDBOOK.md) for the common rhythm.

## The agreement in practice

| Topic | Working expectation |
| --- | --- |
| Term | Six months, with a documented Month-3 review |
| Time | Usually 10–15 hours/week including preparation and follow-up |
| Support | Curriculum, facilitation support, peer cohort, and an agreed program contact |
| Reporting | A session log within 48 hours and [monthly update](monthly-report-template.md) by the 5th |
| Delivery | Eight session credits/month averaged across a quarter, with outcomes and quality considered alongside credits |
| Exit | Reusable materials, a portfolio, and a successor or next-step plan where practical |

## Session credits

Credits compare preparation and depth without pretending all gatherings are alike. The quarterly target is **24 credits**. A short month is normal; do not compress or invent sessions to chase a monthly number.

| Session type | Minimum length | Minimum participants | Credits |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Structured cohort session | 90 minutes | 3 | 1.0 |
| Hands-on workshop with a practical outcome | 120 minutes | 5 | 1.5 |
| Community meetup or reading club | 60 minutes | 4 | 0.75 |
| Train-the-trainer session | 90 minutes | 2 | 1.5 |
| Scheduled 1:1 mentoring with an agenda | 45 minutes | 1 | 0.25 |
| Technical public talk | 30 minutes | 20 | 1.0 |

Guardrails: 1:1 mentoring is capped at 2 credits/month; aim for at least 3 credits/month from structured cohort work across the quarter. If those rules do not fit a high-quality local format, explain why and agree an alternative before reporting it.

## Outcomes over activity

By the end of each quarter, aim to document at least two participant milestones: completing a multi-week track, opening a first PR, operating useful infrastructure, beginning to facilitate, or applying to a relevant opportunity. Attendance is context, not the goal. Do not identify participants publicly without their consent.

## Materials and curriculum

Use and adapt the [Code Orange curriculum](https://github.com/code-orange-dev/curriculum), which is CC0. Publish new reusable materials or translations when safe, either to the curriculum repo or another agreed location. Follow licenses and attribution rules for external materials.

## Boundaries

The fellowship is not employment and does not require a fellow to be an expert in every topic. Never improvise beyond your confidence in a way that puts participants at risk; say what you do not know, follow up, and bring in an expert where needed. Discuss pauses, scope changes, and private reporting early.
