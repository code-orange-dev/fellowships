# Educator Fellowship

> Help a community learn Bitcoin through a consistent, safe, and repeatable format.

## Good fit

You can name the people you will serve, a format you can sustain, topics you can teach confidently, and a plausible first session or event. You do not need to be a developer. You do need 10–15 reliable hours/week and the humility to say when you need help.

Formats can include cohorts, study clubs, hands-on workshops, and local-language content. The goal is not a busy calendar; it is people returning, gaining capability, and eventually helping the next group learn.

## The deal

| You receive | You commit to |
| --- | --- |
| $256/month in Bitcoin, subject to a signed agreement | A realistic teaching plan and dependable delivery |
| Curriculum, facilitation support, and peer feedback | Session logs within 48 hours and a monthly update by the 5th |
| Help documenting the program and training a successor | A Month-3 review and Month-6 handoff/portfolio |

The normal target is eight session credits/month, averaged across a quarter. See the [credit guide](README-DETAIL.md).

## The arc

**Month 1:** launch. **Months 2–3:** establish a rhythm and learn what participants need. **Month 3:** adapt delivery or support. **Months 4–5:** improve the learning journey and train help. **Month 6:** leave reusable materials, a [portfolio](../templates/graduation-portfolio.md), and a next owner or next step.
