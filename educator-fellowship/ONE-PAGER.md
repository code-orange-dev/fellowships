# Educator Fellowship - One Pager

> $256/month, 6 months (extendable), 10-15 hrs/week. Get paid to build the Bitcoin dev community in your city.

---

## The Deal

| | |
|---|---|
| **Stipend** | $256/month, paid monthly on reliable rails |
| **Duration** | 6 months, extendable for proven educators |
| **Commitment** | 10-15 focused hours per week, alongside any day job |
| **Support** | Curriculum (CC0, ready to run), facilitation coaching, event support, hardware for IRL sessions |
| **Community** | Bi-weekly fellow sync, Code Orange Discord, optional Bali hub access |
| **Outcome** | 30+ participants reached, one successor trained, reusable materials on our GitHub |

---

## Pick Your Format (or combine)

- **Run a cohort** - Bitcoin Dojo (7wk), Decoding Bitcoin (8wk), Privacy Track (12 sessions), rawBit (10wk), or propose your own
- **Host a club** - PR Review Club, Optech Deep Dive, Reading Club, Rust for Bitcoiners. The key word is *consistently*
- **Host IRL events** - minimum twice a month: self-custody, mining (BitAxe), nodes, privacy workshops, hackathons, meetups
- **Create content** - tutorials, videos, exercises, translations into Bahasa Indonesia / Thai / Vietnamese. 2 substantial pieces per month, published CC0

---

## Who Should Apply

- You know Bitcoin well enough to teach it - from a Code Orange cohort, meetup leadership, or deep personal study
- You care about getting more people into Bitcoin and want to do it seriously, not as a side hobby
- You can protect 10-15 hours a week for 6 months, honestly
- Consistency over charisma: a decent session every week beats a brilliant one twice

---

## What You Can Expect From Us

- Ready-to-run open-source curriculum, facilitation coaching, and honest feedback on your sessions
- Help with venues and hardware (nodes, signing devices, BitAxes) where we can
- Public amplification of your events and materials
- A clear arc: setup (M1) -> rhythm (M2-3) -> growth (M4-5) -> handoff + final report (M6)
- A path into the Developer Track if teaching makes you want to build instead

---

## What We Expect From You

- **8 session credits per month**, averaged across the quarter - full credit table in the [detailed spec](README-DETAIL.md), every session logged within 48h
- **Two participant outcomes per quarter** - at least two people reach a real milestone: first PR opened, cohort completed, node deployed, or they start facilitating themselves. Activity without progression is a failed quarter
- **Monthly report** by the 5th ([template](../templates/monthly-report.md))
- **Weekly public signal** - event recaps, session notes, or progress posts people can follow
- **Documentation** - write everything down so someone else can run your initiative after you
- **Train your successor** - the initiative should survive you

---

## How It Can End Early

- No report and no sessions for 2 consecutive weeks without cause
- No participant outcomes by the end of Month 3 after coaching support
- Repeated no-shows to sessions you announced
