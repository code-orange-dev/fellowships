# Developer Fellowship - One Pager

> $512/month, 6 months (extendable), 15-20 hrs/week. Get paid to ship Bitcoin open source.

---

## The Deal

| | |
|---|---|
| **Stipend** | $512/month, paid monthly on reliable rails |
| **Duration** | 6 months, extendable for proven contributors |
| **Commitment** | 15-20 focused hours per week, alongside any day job |
| **Support** | Named mentor, weekly call, PR review within ~48h |
| **Community** | Bi-weekly fellow sync, Code Orange Discord, optional Bali hub access |
| **Outcome** | 6+ PRs merged, one grant application submitted, path to independent FOSS work |

---

## Who Should Apply

- You completed a Bitcoin dev cohort (Code Orange, Chaincode, Summer of Bitcoin, or serious self-study)
- You can already write code and navigate a real codebase - we are de-risking, not teaching basics
- You have visible GitHub activity - even one small merged PR counts
- You can protect 15-20 hours a week for 6 months, honestly
- You are committed to Bitcoin specifically, not using this as a stepping stone to generic Web3 work

Fastest path in: open at least one PR to your target project *before* applying.

---

## What You Can Expect From Us

- A named mentor matched to your target project, committed for the full 6 months
- A defined project and work plan agreed in Month 1 - no wandering
- Timely reviews and honest feedback on every PR
- Public amplification of your work - your GitHub profile is yours, we help people see it
- A clear arc: setup (M1) -> steady shipping (M2-3) -> deep work (M4-5) -> land it + grant application (M6)
- Payment on time, every month - for a Global South fellow, reliability is the point

---

## What We Expect From You

- **6+ PRs opened, ~3-4+ merged** to your target project over 6 months (merges depend on maintainers; quality and consistency are what you control)
- **Weekly public status update** - short blog post, Nostr note, or GitHub discussion thread. This is non-negotiable: it is our proof of work and your professional footprint
- **Weekly mentor call** - show up prepared; escalate honestly when blocked for more than a week
- **Monthly report** by the 5th ([template](../templates/monthly-report.md))
- **Reviews, not just code** - from Month 4, review other contributors' PRs regularly
- **One knowledge-transfer artifact** - a blog post, workshop, or curriculum improvement teaching what you learned. This is how one fellow compounds into ten future students

---

## How It Can End Early

- No status update and no check-in for 2 consecutive weeks without cause
- Zero PR activity by end of Month 2 (Month 1 is grace, Month 2 is evidence)
- Work you cannot explain when asked - one warning, then done

---

## Apply

Send a focused proposal (use the [template](../templates/developer-proposal.md)) with your GitHub, target project, and 6-month plan - any of three ways:

- PR it to `applications/<your-github-handle>.md`
- Post in `#fellowships` on [Discord](https://discord.gg/xd6dmPF9bA)
- Email fellowship@codeorange.dev, subject "Developer Fellowship - [Your Name]"

Rolling review, response within 2 weeks. Full details: [Developer Track](README.md).
